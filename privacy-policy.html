Rupai Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Rupai is developed and maintained by an independent developer, referred to here as “we”. This policy explains what data remains on your device and what is shared with third-party services.

1. Your data stays on your device

Rupai has no accounts and no servers. What you add is stored only on your phone, and we never receive a copy.

If you delete the app or lose your phone, your data is gone. Use Export my data in Settings to keep a copy.

2. Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information. It never receives the data you add to the app.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings under Google › Ads.

3. Your choices

  • Export your data from Settings › Privacy & data.
  • Delete everything with Start fresh, or by uninstalling the app.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts or location, and we do not sell personal information. Rupai is not for children under 13.

4. Contact

Questions, or changes to this policy? Email ahssoftwares.ai@gmail.com.
